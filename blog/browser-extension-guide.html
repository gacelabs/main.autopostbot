How to Install and Use the AutoPostBot Browser Extension to Collect Shopee and Amazon Products

Tutorial · 8 min read · By the AutoPostBot Team

On this pageWhy the Extension MattersWhat You Need Before You StartInstalling the ExtensionCollecting Products Step by StepTips for Cleaner ResultsTroubleshootingUsing the Extension ResponsiblyQuick Recap

The browser extension is the part of the workflow that saves you the most time. Instead of copying product details by hand, you open a product in your browser, use the extension, and send the information to your AutoPostBot dashboard. This tutorial covers installation, daily use, and tips for clean results.

Why the Extension Matters

Collecting products manually is slow and error-prone. It is easy to miss an image, mistype a price, or copy the wrong link. The extension handles that first step: it reads the product information displayed on the page you choose and sends it to your account, where you manage it and complete the publishing workflow.

The extension never publishes anything on its own. Publishing happens in the web app, where you review and approve every post.

What You Need Before You Start

  • An active AutoPostBot subscription.
  • A supported browser, [list supported browsers].
  • An affiliate account with the shop you want to use, such as Shopee or Amazon.
  • A Facebook Page connected in the web app, if you plan to publish.

Installing the Extension

  1. After subscribing, open the installation link in the onboarding screen, or use the official link on our How It Works page, [Extension store link].
  2. Choose to add the extension to your browser.
  3. Review the permissions your browser lists. The extension is designed to work on shop pages you select, not to read your general browsing activity.
  4. Pin the extension icon to your toolbar so it is always one click away.
  5. Sign in to the extension with your AutoPostBot account, [confirm sign-in flow].

If you do not see the icon after installing, open your browser's extensions menu and pin it from there.

Collecting Products Step by Step

1. Select your affiliate shop. Open the shop you want to work with, such as Shopee or Amazon, in a normal browser tab.

2. Open the product you want to promote. Start from the specific product page, where the title, images, and price are displayed.

3. Use the extension to scrape the product information. Click the extension icon and follow the on-screen action, [exact button label]. It collects what the page shows, such as the title, images, price, shop name, and product link.

4. Send the data to AutoPostBot. Confirm that you want to send the product to your web app. It should appear in your dashboard within moments.

5. Manage and publish in the web app. Find the imported product, polish the content, and publish it to your connected Facebook Page.

Tips for Cleaner Results

  • Wait for the page to finish loading. If images or prices are still loading, the extension may collect incomplete information.
  • Collect from product pages, not search results. Search pages show summaries, while product pages contain the full details.
  • Check your affiliate link. Make sure the link credits your affiliate account, and fix it in the web app if it does not.
  • Verify prices and availability before publishing. An outdated price frustrates your audience and damages trust.
  • Work in batches. Collect a group of related products in one session, then edit and publish in another.

Troubleshooting

The extension does not collect any information. Refresh the page, make sure you are on an individual product page, and try again. Shops occasionally change their page layouts, which can affect how information is read.

The product does not show up in my dashboard. Check that you are signed in to the same AutoPostBot account in the extension and the web app. Then check your connection and send the product again.

Some details are missing or wrong. You can edit any imported product in the web app before publishing. If a problem keeps happening on a specific shop page, tell us through the Contact page.

Using the Extension Responsibly

The extension is meant for collecting products for your own affiliate marketing. Follow each shop's affiliate program rules, and do not use it to bulk-harvest data or overload any site. Our Terms of Service explain acceptable use in more detail.

Quick Recap

Install the extension, select a shop, scrape the product, send it to AutoPostBot, then manage and publish in the web app. After two or three rounds, the process takes a fraction of the time it took by hand.
