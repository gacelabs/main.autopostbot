Getting Started with AutoPostBot: From Subscription to Your First Facebook Post

Whole Page

If you create affiliate content, you know the routine: open a shop, copy the product title, save the images, grab your affiliate link, write a caption, paste everything into Facebook, then repeat for the next product. It works, but it eats hours you could spend creating better content and growing your audience.

AutoPostBot replaces that routine with one connected workflow. This guide walks you from your first subscription to your first published post.

What AutoPostBot Does

AutoPostBot is a web application that helps content creators manage affiliate product content and social media publishing from one place. It has two parts that work together:

  • The web app, where you manage content, connect social accounts, and publish.
  • The browser extension, which collects product information from your chosen affiliate shop and sends it to the web app.

Today, AutoPostBot supports Shopee and Amazon affiliate product content, plus Custom Content you write yourself. For publishing, it supports Facebook Pages. Temu, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, and other integrations are planned and marked "Coming soon" on our site. They are not available yet.

Step 1: Choose a Plan and Create Your Account

Start on the Pricing page and pick the plan that fits your workload. Then register with your name, email address, and a strong, unique password. This matters because your account will be connected to your Facebook Pages.

After you subscribe, you will be guided to install the browser extension. Do not skip this. The extension is how products get into your dashboard, which makes it an essential companion to the web app.

Step 2: Install the Browser Extension

Follow the installation link shown after you subscribe, [Extension store link], and add the extension to your browser. Review the permissions your browser lists and approve only what the extension requests. Pin it to your toolbar so it is easy to reach while you browse a shop. Our extension tutorial covers installation and daily use in detail.

Step 3: Connect Your Facebook Page

In the web app, open your connections settings, [exact menu label], and choose to connect a Facebook Page. You will be taken to Facebook to Sign in and choose which Pages AutoPostBot may access. Keep in mind:

  • You must be an admin of the Page you want to connect.
  • AutoPostBot never receives your Facebook password. You grant access through Facebook's own permission screen.
  • You can disconnect a Page at any time in AutoPostBot or in your Facebook settings.

Step 4: Collect Your First Products

Open your affiliate shop, such as a Shopee or Amazon product page. Use the extension to scrape the product information and send it to your web app. The title, images, price, and link will appear in your dashboard, ready to manage. Start small: collect three to five products you genuinely like and would recommend.

Step 5: Review, Edit, and Publish

Open the imported content in the web app and turn the raw product data into a post your audience will want to read. Check the details, because prices and availability change. Edit the caption so it sounds like you, add your own experience, and make sure your affiliate disclosure is clear. Then choose your connected Facebook Page, publish, and check your Page to confirm the post looks right.

Bonus: Create Custom Content

Not every post needs to be a product post. Use Custom Content to write your own updates, tips, announcements, or reviews, and manage them in the same dashboard as your affiliate posts. A healthy Page mixes recommendations with content that is purely useful or personal.

Common First-Week Questions

Do I need the extension for Custom Content? No. The extension is for collecting affiliate products. Custom Content is created directly in the web app.

Can I publish to Instagram or Threads? Not yet. Those are marked "Coming soon." Facebook Pages is the publishing platform available today.

What if a product page does not scrape correctly? Shop sites change their layouts from time to time. Refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, contact support through the Contact page.

What to Do Next

You now have the basic loop: install, connect, collect, edit, publish. Next, read our extension guide for faster collecting, and our article on writing affiliate posts people trust.
